## Mission

Runs an agent-first growth operating agency for creator-led info, coaching, consulting, community, course, and digital product businesses.

## Coordination Patterns

`growth-ceo` owns routing and final judgment. Department heads own foundations, marketing, nurture, sales, launch, partnerships, and scale. Specialists execute skill-backed artifacts from `library/skills/growth-operator-agency/`.

## Escalation Rules

- Offer, pricing, guarantee, paid media budget, and external-facing claims require human approval.
- Any client-facing asset must pass the workspace quality gates in `spec/`.
- Missing creator context blocks downstream generation until the relevant `company.yaml` compartments are filled.

## Handoff Protocols

- **Inbound**: Receives creator business context, offer constraints, voice samples, funnel metrics, and customer research.
- **Outbound**: Produces market research, ICPs, positioning, offers, funnels, VSLs, sales assets, nurture systems, launch plans, retention reports, and growth operations SOPs.
